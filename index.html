Building across borders

I'm Meric. American, living in London for now. I built Global Tether Ventures so my curiosity never has to settle on one thing.

It started with dogs

I was in college and started walking dogs on the side. Little did I know it would follow me so closely. Ten years later, five of them fully nomadic, I'm still traveling around to watch pets while their owners are away.

nomadicdogsitter.com

Then I moved to London

After almost a decade in NYC I was ready for a new chapter, so I moved. As soon as I landed, the bureaucracy of multiple currencies became a real bottleneck. So I built Cluster as a financial mission control, because when you live in more than one country, so do your assets.

clusterfinancial.com

But I couldn't sit still

I always wanted to travel, and living in London was the perfect excuse, so I booked my first flight. As I explored, I was drawn to the moments that make a place feel like nowhere else. Citizen of the World is my attempt to share them, in short clips and vignettes.

Watch it on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

citizenoftheworld.club

I'm still exploring

Who knows what's next.
